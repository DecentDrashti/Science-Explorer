Science, Curiosity,
and Everyday Life

Every great discovery began with a simple question. When Newton watched an apple fall, when Curie studied invisible rays, or when Darwin observed animals on distant islands — curiosity was the spark that changed our world.

Science is not just something that happens in laboratories. It is in the food we eat, the devices we use, and the air we breathe. Understanding science means understanding the world around us more deeply.
