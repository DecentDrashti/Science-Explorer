Question 1 of 5

Which planet is known as the Red Planet?

Reveal the correct answer
✓ Mars is known as the Red Planet. Its surface is covered in iron oxide (rust), giving it a distinctive reddish appearance.

Question 2 of 5

What is the basic unit of life?

Reveal the correct answer
✓ Cell is the basic unit of life. All living organisms are made up of cells — the smallest unit that can carry out all the functions of life.

Question 3 of 5

Which gas do plants use during photosynthesis?

Reveal the correct answer
✓ Carbon dioxide (CO₂) is used by plants during photosynthesis. They take it in through tiny pores called stomata and use it, along with water and sunlight, to make glucose.

Question 4 of 5

What force pulls objects towards Earth?

Reveal the correct answer
✓ Gravity is the force that pulls objects towards Earth. Isaac Newton described this force after observing a falling apple — the same force that keeps the planets in orbit around the Sun.

Question 5 of 5

Which is the largest planet in our solar system?

Reveal the correct answer
✓ Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system. It is so large that more than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it.
